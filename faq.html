2026 Faculty Survey

Frequently Asked Questions

Who should complete the survey?

The survey is intended for current college and university faculty who have been contacted with an invitation to participate. If you have taught within the last two years, or are otherwise active on campus in a faculty role, you are eligible to participate. Along with tenured and tenure-track faculty, this includes adjunct faculty and lecturers. Individuals who are now fully retired or no longer meaningfully involved with their institution may disregard the invitation. If you received an invitation email by accident and are not a faculty member, you may disregard the message and/or click the opt-out link in the message.

I’m an adjunct/part-time/lecturer. Does my perspective still count?

Yes. Adjunct, clinical, lecturers, and part-time faculty are all in scope. If you are still unsure, feel free to reply to the invitation email to check.

When does the survey close?

The survey is anticipated to close in January 2027.

Who is running this survey? Who is the PI?

The 2026 FIRE Faculty Survey was designed by Nathan Honeycutt, Ph.D. at the Foundation for Individual Rights and Expression. He is responsible for all aspects of the study, including (but not limited to) fielding/data collection, analysis, and authoring a report detailing the results.

Which IRB approved this study?

The study was reviewed and approved by the University of Arkansas Institutional Review Board on June 11, 2026.

Can I receive the results?

Thank you for your interest. Yes. Please provide your email address here and a copy of the report will be shared once available.

How did you get my name?

Contact information was compiled from publicly available directories at your institution. You are being contacted because when this information was collected it appeared that you were an active faculty member at one of the institutions selected for recruitment.

Is participation voluntary?

Yes. Participants can stop at any time.

How long does it take?

It is expected that your participation will take about 15 minutes.

Can I skip questions?

Yes.

When and where will results be published?

A report detailing the findings from the survey will be published by FIRE once available. Academic pre-print(s) and/or paper(s) may follow.

Who do I contact if I encounter a technical problem?

Please reply to the survey invitation you received and briefly describe the issue you’re experiencing.

I don’t know the answer to some questions. Should I still participate?

Not every question will apply equally to every faculty member. Please answer the questions to the best of your ability. Complete submissions are preferred, but questions can be skipped as needed.
