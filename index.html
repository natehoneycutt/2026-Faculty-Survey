About the study

The goal of this research study is to better understand faculty experiences on campus, particularly related to the topics of free speech and academic freedom, as well as their perspectives on key issues facing higher education. For this study faculty at over 400 U.S. colleges and universities are being contacted with an invitation to participate. The survey takes about 15 minutes to complete and will close in January 2027.
